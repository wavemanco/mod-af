# dynamics — backlog

Work consciously postponed, each item with a concrete specification and the event that makes it due.
Nothing here is built until its trigger fires. Known limits of what is built are in
[`REVIEW-LOG.md`](./REVIEW-LOG.md) ("Revisit when…").

**Filing.** Search this table first (open and closed rows) so an item is not filed twice. A new item
takes the next `DYN-` number, which is never reused or renumbered. Rows are never deleted: a finished
item is marked `DONE YYYY-MM-DD · <test that fails without it>`, a dropped one
`DECLINED YYYY-MM-DD · <reason>`.

| DYN-001 | **Two speeds: a quick sketch beside the full verified run** ("option (d)" when stockflow and the dynamics prototype were merged in October 2026: one plugin with a light, unchecked mode beside the full one) | `/dynamics sketch <what to model> — <question>`. One combined survey pass (no separate angles), no review rounds unless asked. The model carries `mode: "sketch"`; the check then refuses any part marked *observed* (no part is observed; *intended* and *missing* are allowed with a claim) and still enforces the structure rules (flows, signs, loops and their types, units, empty-stock guards). The page shows a banner from the model, not typed by hand: "Sketch: not checked against the sources; every part is a modelled guess". `/dynamics verify <model.json>` upgrades a sketch: it runs the full method on it, keeps the ids, and a part becomes observed only with a checked quote. The guard reads the parts themselves, not a flag. In any mode, the check warns when no part of the whole model is observed or carries evidence (a sketch has none by construction), and about each loop in `answer.loops` with no observed part and no part with evidence; the page marks that loop "every part a modelled guess". Deleting `mode` from a sketch therefore removes the banner but not these warnings and marks, even when `answer.loops` is empty; only checked evidence removes them. (This also flags a full run whose answer rests on pure guesses.) Tests: the banner appears exactly when `mode` is sketch; an observed part in a sketch is an error; a verified upgrade keeps every id; a sketch with `mode` deleted still gets the warning and the mark on every answer loop, and the model-wide warning when `answer.loops` is empty. Target: under about a third of a full run's time and tokens on a mid-size repository (measured, and stated in the README). | Three separate requests for a faster or cheaper map, each tallied with a link in this row's Status cell, **or** a median full run over 15 minutes of wall-clock time or $5 across three runs on real targets, read from the runs' records (the method records minutes and cost when the environment shows them; runs without them are not counted). | OPEN · requests 0/3 |
| DYN-002 | **Stages: watch the system grow** (the prototype's stage selector) | The model gains an ordered `stages` list (id, label, the evidence that defines each: a phase, version or roadmap entry; configuration layers only when labelled as layers, not time) and an optional `stage` on each element. | DYN-003 | **What drives this, and what does this drive** (inspired by Archify's upstream/downstream reach) | In the detail panel of a selected element, two buttons with counts: "What drives this" and "What this drives". Each highlights everything that reaches the element (or that it reaches) along links and flows, and fades the rest, derived from the model, never from the drawing. Tests: the reached set equals a graph search on the model for the worked example; a figure with no path shows zero. | A model with more than 20 elements in one figure, or a reviewer or reader asking what affects a given element. | OPEN |
| DYN-004 | **How does A affect B?** (inspired by Archify's route probe) | Pick two elements; the page lists each causal path from A to B, at most 20 (then "and n more"), with its net sign (the product of the step signs, a flow counting + into the stock it fills and − out of the one it drains: more A means more B, or less B), its length and any delay, and highlights the paths. Paths with opposite net signs are flagged as "pulls both ways", which is where loop dominance matters. Derived from the model only. Tests: the worked example's paths and signs match a hand count; opposite-sign paths are flagged; the cap holds. | A question of the form "does X help or hurt Y?", or a review finding that a reader could not tell how two parts relate. | OPEN |
| DYN-005 | **Step through a loop** (inspired by Archify's explainer, steps beside the diagram) | Selecting a loop offers "walk through": each step of its story is shown beside the figure while the step it describes is highlighted, one at a time, ending with the loop type and why (the count of − steps: − links, and flows out of the stock they drain). Tests: the steps follow the loop's order, every step, link or flow, appears once, and a loop that balances only through a drain is explained as balancing. | A cold-reader finding that a loop's story is hard to follow, or a loop longer than five links in a delivered model. | OPEN |
